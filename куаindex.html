The Language of Hands

One gesture — a world of meanings.
How the same hand sign can wish luck, start a fight, or threaten someone.

Gesture 01 / 03

Crossed Fingers: Luck or Insult?

🤞

In the UK and USA, crossing fingers means "Good luck" or cancelling a promise. But in Vietnam, this exact gesture is extremely rude, equivalent to the middle finger.

A simple wish for luck in London becomes a serious insult in Hanoi.

Gesture 02 / 03

Finger Purse: Argument or Pause?

🤌

In Italy, the pinched fingers mean "What do you want?!" — a confrontational gesture used in arguments.

Yet in India, the exact same hand shape means "Wait a moment" — polite and calm.

Gesture 03 / 03

The Fig: Good Luck or Rude?

🤏

In Brazil and Portugal, the fig sign brings good luck and wards off the evil eye.

But in Turkey and Russia, it is a rude refusal equal to the middle finger.

🌍

Remember: Hands Speak Loudly

A gesture that feels natural at home can offend, confuse, or threaten someone on the other side of the world.

Before you wave, point, or raise a finger abroad — ask yourself what it really means.
